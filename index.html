The SHAMEGRIN story

For people who

Gary Makinson is the Canadian artist behind SHAMEGRIN — a music project combining dark emo rap, emotional rock, alternative hip hop and cinematic storytelling.

SHAMEGRIN is built around raw, confessional songs shaped by anxiety, heartbreak, loneliness, identity and survival. The music is made for people who recognize the feeling behind the smile.

There is no need for a manufactured image. The music is the identity. SHAMEGRIN is the sound behind the smile — raw, personal, dark and honest.
