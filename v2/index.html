The story

For people who
smile while
breaking

Gary Makinson built SHAMEGRIN from real nights with anxiety — the racing thoughts, the tight chest, the feeling that you're falling apart while everyone still sees you holding it together. He doesn't write party anthems. He writes survival songs.

Every track is for someone who needed to hear "you're not crazy for feeling this" at the hour when therapists are asleep and your friends think you're okay. Dark emo rap, spoken word, cinematic confession — music that names the fear instead of hiding it.

That's why there's no face on camera. Anxiety doesn't always look like sadness. Sometimes it looks like a smile. SHAMEGRIN is the sound behind that smile — so you know you're not alone in the war inside your head.

— Gary Makinson · SHAMEGRIN

FAQ

Who is SHAMEGRIN?

SHAMEGRIN is the music project of Canadian artist Gary Makinson — dark emo rap, spoken word, and cinematic 4K music videos. 100K+ monthly streams, 71+ official videos, and a global underground audience finding the sound at 3am.

Why does Gary write music for people with anxiety?

Gary built SHAMEGRIN from real nights with anxiety — racing thoughts, tight chest, smiling while breaking. The songs name the fear when nobody has the words, especially at 3am when the world is asleep.

Where can I stream SHAMEGRIN?

Stream on Spotify & Apple Music, SoundCloud, and YouTube. Start with Outrun the Crash or The Best of SHAMEGRIN playlist.

What is Outrun the Crash about?

The flagship SHAMEGRIN anthem about survival: "I don't wanna die — I just don't wanna feel like this anymore." Cinematic 4K dark emo rap for anyone who needed to hear it at 3am.
