About the music

This is what
the grin
sounds like

SHAMEGRIN is dark emo rap that says the thing you’d never say out loud. Gary Makinson writes it, records it, and produces it in Canada — late-night drums, spoken-word edges, cinematic videos. No face. Just sound.

The songs are nights, not playlists. Panic in the chest. Money stress. Memory that won’t sit down. A smile in the grocery line while everything inside is loud. This is not party music. This is the track you put on when the house is quiet and your head isn’t.

The Stutter Boys and the ShameGrin is the 2026 flagship — 3:54 of confession with the grin still on. Around it: The Wolf That Stays Caged (the feeling you lock in), Tired of This World (the walk-home ache), Fall Into the Ground (when you want the floor to take you), The Mountains Still Call to Me (the place you keep going back to), Oblivion Home No More, Anime Star. Deeper in the vault: Dragged Through Heaven and Hell, Bleed, Bite Marks, After the Fire, Outrun the Crash.

Answers

FAQ

Who is SHAMEGRIN?

SHAMEGRIN is the music project of Canadian artist Gary Makinson — dark emo rap, spoken word, and cinematic music videos for people with anxiety.

What is the new single?

The Stutter Boys and the ShameGrin — a 2026 single (3:54) out now on Spotify. Stream it here or open the full album page on Spotify.

Where can I stream SHAMEGRIN?

Start on Spotify and SoundCloud (shamegrin). Videos are on YouTube @SHAMEGRIN. Buy the album on ElasticStage.

Why “No face. Just sound”?

Because anxiety doesn’t always look like sadness. Sometimes it looks like a smile. The music is the part you can’t see.

Is SHAMEGRIN on TikTok?

Yes — follow @shamegrin for clips, drops, and repost editions.

Where do I buy the SHAMEGRIN album?

Buy the ShameGrin 12″ vinyl on ElasticStage: elasticstage.com/soundcloud/releases/gary-makinson-shamegrin-album. Checkout and shipping are on ElasticStage. This site does not run its own cart.

What is SHAMEGRIN’s SoundCloud?

soundcloud.com/shamegrin — the full independent catalog, including Dragged Through Heaven and Hell, Everyone Has a Scar, All the Faces That Turned, Bleed, Bite Marks, and After the Fire.

Is Gary Makinson the same person as SHAMEGRIN?

Yes. Gary Makinson is the Canadian artist. SHAMEGRIN is the project name. Same writer, same voice, same grin.
